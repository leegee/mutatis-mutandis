Does the geographical density of luminous/fair mythological descriptors correlate with the distribution of light pigmentation, after controlling for Indo-European language, chronology and cultural transmission?

| Period               | Region                                 | Evidence for light/blond phenotype                                             | Relevance to our motif                                    |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mesolithic           | Scandinavia / Baltic                   | Light eyes already occur; hair generally more variable/dark                    | Northern light phenotype has deep roots                   |
| Neolithic            | Britain                                | Some light pigmentation, but many early British farmers were darker-haired     | Important: **not simply a primordial blond Britain**      |
| Copper Age           | Eastern/Central Europe                 | Light hair still relatively uncommon overall                                   | Transitional                                              |
| **Early Bronze Age** | Britain                                | Increasing light skin/eyes; blond hair appears                                 | Major demographic/phenotypic transition                   |

| Bronze Age           | Denmark / Estonia / Central Europe     | Several individuals with inferred blond hair and light skin                    | Northern/Central European concentration                   |
| Iron Age             | Denmark / Finland                      | Blond/light + blue eyes particularly visible                                   | Strongest northern correspondence                         |
| Iron Age             | Italy                                  | Some light-haired individuals occur                                            | Important counterexample to a simple north/south division |
