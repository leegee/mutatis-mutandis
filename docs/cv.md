## Pre-Doctoral Research

**Event-based Semantic Analysis of Early Modern English** - December 2025–present

Independent pre-doctoral research developing an event-based framework for historical semantics, working with the EEBO-TCP corpus (1625–1665) and MacBERTh, a BERT-style language model trained on early modern English. The project treats each token occurrence as a discrete semantic event with full corpus provenance, modelling meaning as a distribution over contextual neighbourhood fields rather than through aggregated word vectors.

Full end-to-end pipeline implemented: contextual event extraction and Zarr-based storage; FAISS-based ANN indexing and neighbourhood retrieval; interactive dual-mode visualisation supporting both aggregated and raw event-level exploration, with full traceability to source documents.

Technical stack: Python (PyTorch, NumPy, FAISS), PostgreSQL/PostGIS, SolidJS, TypeScript, D3, Cosmos.gl.
