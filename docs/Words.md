## Germanic

| Language           | Form          | Basic sense          | Extended/important senses                                            | Particularly relevant                                                           |
| ------------------ | ------------- | -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Old Norse**      | **hvítr**     | white                | bright, fair, shining; white/light hair                              | central “white” term; Baldr, Heimdallr, white animals                           |
| Old Norse          | **fagr**      | beautiful, fair      | handsome, lovely                                                     | often overlaps with whiteness/brightness in descriptions of supernatural beings |
| Old Norse          | **bjartr**    | bright, shining      | brilliant, radiant, fair                                             | Baldr; luminous supernatural appearance                                         |
| Old Norse          | **ljóss**     | light, luminous      | bright, clear; light-coloured                                        | ljósálfar (“light elves”)                                                       |
| Old Norse          | **bleikr**    | pale, light-coloured | pallid, yellowish/pale                                               | potentially important for bodily pallor                                         |
| Old Norse          | **fǫlr**      | pale, pallid         | wan, yellowish                                                       | potentially pathological/negative whiteness                                     |
| Old Norse          | **svartr**    | black                | dark, gloomy; metaphorically hostile/evil                            | principal opposite of *hvítr*                                                   |
| Old Norse          | **døkkr**     | dark                 | obscure, gloomy                                                      | *dökkálfar*, “dark elves”                                                       |
| Old Norse          | **blár**      | blue                 | dark, blue-black, livid                                              | especially important with ravens and weapons; **not simply modern blue**        |
| Old Norse          | **rauðr**     | red                  | blood-red, fiery; golden in some contexts                            | blood, wounds, fire; third member of some black/white/red contrasts             |
| **Old English**    | **hwīt**      | white                | bright, shining, radiant                                             | cognate with ON *hvítr*                                                         |
| Old English        | **fæger**     | fair, beautiful      | handsome, pleasing                                                   | semantic equivalent of ON *fagr*                                                |
| Old English        | **beorht**    | bright               | shining, splendid, glorious                                          | important luminous/fair vocabulary                                              |
| Old English        | **lēoht**     | light                | luminous, bright                                                     | semantic counterpart to ON *ljóss*                                              |
| Old English        | **blāc**      | pale/white           | pale, wan; sometimes black/dark depending on lexical history/context | potentially important for bodily colour                                         |
| Old English        | **blāt**      | livid, pale          | ghastly, corpse-like                                                 | particularly interesting for pathological/unnatural pallor                      |
| Old English        | **swart**     | black, dark          | dusky, swarthy                                                       | cognate with ON *svartr*                                                        |
| Old English        | **dēorc**     | dark                 | gloomy, obscure                                                      | cognate semantic field with ON *døkkr*                                          |
| Old English        | **read**      | red                  | blood-red, ruddy                                                     | blood, weapons, fire                                                            |
| **Proto-Germanic** | ***hwītaz***  | white                | —                                                                    | ancestor of ON *hvítr*, OE *hwīt*                                               |
| Proto-Germanic     | ***fagraz***  | fair, beautiful      | —                                                                    | ancestor of ON *fagr*, OE *fæger*                                               |
| Proto-Germanic     | ***berhtaz*** | bright               | shining                                                              | ancestor of ON *bjartr*, OE *beorht*                                            |
| Proto-Germanic     | ***leuhtaz*** | light                | luminous                                                             | ancestor of ON *ljóss*, OE *lēoht*                                              |
| Proto-Germanic     | ***swartaz*** | black                | dark                                                                 | ancestor of ON *svartr*, OE *swart*                                             |
| Proto-Germanic     | ***rauthaz*** | red                  | —                                                                    | ancestor of ON *rauðr*, OE *read*                                               |


## Celtic

| Language            | Form                   | Basic sense   | Extended/important senses                   | Particularly relevant                       |
| ------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Old Irish**       | **bán**                | white         | fair, pale; later blessed/pure associations | extremely important for white animals       |
| Old Irish           | **find / finn**        | white, fair   | bright, beautiful; fair-haired              | major personal-name element; Fionn          |
| Old Irish           | **geal**               | bright, white | shining, brilliant                          | later strongly associated with brightness   |
| Old Irish           | **derg**               | red           | blood-red, ruddy                            | blood/fire                                  |
| Old Irish           | **dub**                | black         | dark, gloomy                                | major counterpart to *bán*                  |
| Old Irish           | **gorm**               | blue          | sometimes dark/blackish                     | semantic history complicated                |
| Old Irish           | **liath**              | grey          | grey-haired, aged                           | particularly relevant to bodily appearance  |
| Old Irish           | **fionn**              | white/fair    | bright, fair-haired                         | variant/continuation of *find*              |
| **Middle Irish**    | **bán**                | white         | pale, fair                                  | continues directly from Old Irish           |
| Middle Irish        | **find**               | white/fair    | beautiful, bright                           | continues the older semantic complex        |
| **Scottish Gaelic** | **bàn**                | white         | fair, pale; fair-haired                     | direct descendant of Old Irish *bán*        |
| Scottish Gaelic     | **fionn**              | white/fair    | fair-haired, bright                         | direct continuation of *find/finn*          |
| Scottish Gaelic     | **geal**               | white/bright  | brilliant, shining                          | especially luminous whiteness               |
| Scottish Gaelic     | **dubh**               | black         | dark, gloomy                                | counterpart of *bàn*                        |
| **Welsh**           | **gwyn**               | white         | fair, blessed, fortunate                    | extraordinarily important semantic range    |
| Welsh               | **gwen**               | white/fair    | feminine form; fair/beautiful               | personal names and supernatural figures     |
| Welsh               | **lliw**               | colour        | complexion/appearance                       | useful when investigating bodily colour     |
| Welsh               | **du**                 | black         | dark                                        | counterpart to *gwyn*                       |
| Welsh               | **coch**               | red           | ruddy, red                                  | blood/fire                                  |
| **Proto-Celtic**    | ***bandos / *bannos*** | white         | —                                           | proposed source of Goidelic *bán*           |
| Proto-Celtic        | ***windos***           | white, fair   | —                                           | source of *find/fionn* and Brittonic *gwyn* |
