| Hypothesis                                 | Claim                                                                                              | Status                                   |

| **H3 — Indo-European inherited symbolism** | *white / bright / golden / shining* forms an inherited Indo-European mythological semantic complex | **Interesting but unproven**             |

The relevant inherited category may not have been "blond" at all, but something like LUMINOUS / BRIGHT / SHINING, whose physical manifestations include white clothing, white skin, blond/golden hair, radiant weapons, gold, sunlight, etc.
